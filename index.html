Welcome to my Homepage

Hello, I'm Pål. I am a 20-year-old Economics and Business student at Lund University specializing in International Business.

My biggest interest lies in publicly traded securities, where I do a good amount of research on both stocks and specific corporate bonds. Recently, I've become very fascinated with the Japanese market so my research on niche Japanese door handle manufacturers is available here.

I also do think retail stock research is broken. Most platforms are either cluttered, poorly designed, or stripped of critical data. They lack the fast navigation that power users need, forcing investors to pay absurd premiums for traditional terminal interfaces. I’m building Schwerpunkt to change that. It's supposed to be a lean, affordable research platform engineered for both regular investors and power users. It’s currently a Yahoo Finance wrapper as I build out the foundation, but the roadmap is expanding rapidly. Check it out and let me know your thoughts!

Aside from that, I am a big fan of the Austrian school and I'm hoping to land a great job before the AI singularity wipes us all out.
